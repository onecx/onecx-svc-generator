= Architecture Rationale: Why We Use the SVC Generator
:navtitle: SVC Generator Rationale
:description: Explanation of why dedicated scaffolding tools are essential in the era of AI.

In the modern era of generative AI, large language models (LLMs) can easily scaffold a basic Quarkus application within seconds. 
However, building an enterprise-grade microservice that seamlessly integrates into a complex ecosystem requires more than just functional code. 

This page outlines the strategic advantages of using the official *OneCX SVC Generator* over ad-hoc AI-generated scaffolding.

== The Core Dilemma: "Fast Code" vs. "Standardized Architecture"

While AI excel at generating localized business logic, they lack the context, determinism, and governance required to maintain a fleet of hundreds of interconnected microservices. 

The OneCX SVC Generator serves as the architectural foundation, ensuring that every service complies with platform standards from day one.

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===

| Capability | AI-Generated Scaffolding (LLMs) | OneCX SVC Generator

| *Determinism*
| *Probabilistic:* Different prompts or model updates result in varying project layouts, names, and patterns.
| *Deterministic:* Guarantees 100% identical structures, folder layouts, and naming conventions for every run.

| *Ecosystem Knowledge*
| *Generic:* Relies on public training data. Lacks knowledge of internal, proprietary, or highly specific OneCX shared libraries.
| *Deep Integration:* Pre-configured with specialized OneCX components (e.g., tenant extensions, tracing headers, OIDC security).

| *Lifecycle & Governance*
| *One-off:* Cannot easily upgrade existing codebases when platform standards or underlying frameworks change.
| *Maintainable:* Central updates to the generator blueprints scale across the entire platform via schematics or automated upgrades.
|===

== Key Strategic Advantages

=== 1. Absolute Consistency and Platform Compliance
Microservice architectures fail when every service feels like it was written by a different engineering team. 
The SVC Generator enforces strict conventions regarding:

* Base Java package structures (e.g., using the `--package` parameter to isolate enterprise namespaces).
* Unified naming patterns linking the Maven `artifactId` directly to Docker image tags and Helm deployment charts.
* Pre-configured quality gates (SonarQube, Checkstyle) out-of-the-box.

=== 2. Out-of-the-Box OneCX Integration
A generic Quarkus service cannot function within the OneCX universe without manual adjustments. The generator pre-configures:

* *Multi-Tenancy & Security:* Automated wire-up of token propagation and tenant resolution.
* *Observability:* Standardized logging, tracing, and health-check endpoints required by the OneCX central control plane.
* *API-First Tooling:* Hardened configurations for contract-first development using OpenAPI.

=== 3. Continuous Enterprise Governance
When a security vulnerability forces an upgrade (e.g., moving from Quarkus 3 to Quarkus 4, or replacing an expired Maven plugin), patching individual AI-prompted repositories is highly error-prone. 
By centralizing the platform blueprints inside the SVC Generator, the platform team can roll out compliance patches and architecture upgrades deterministically across all teams.

=== 4. Full-Stack Scaffolding (Code to GitOps)
The generator does not stop at the Java layer. It establishes the complete deployment blueprint:

* **CI/CD Pipelines:** Ready-to-use GitLab CI or GitHub Action definitions.
* **Infrastructure as Code:** Helm charts and Kubernetes manifests pre-tailored to the target environment's ingress and routing requirements.

== Conclusion: The Synergistic Developer Workflow

The OneCX SVC Generator and AI do not compete—they complement each other. The recommended workflow leverages the strengths of both tools:

[NOTE]
====
[.text-center]
*The Golden Rule of Platform Development:* +
Use the **Generator** for the structure, security, and pipelines. Use **AI** for the business logic inside the files.
====

=== The Architecture Pipeline

[horizontal,labelwidth=10]

STEP 1::
*The OneCX SVC Generator = The Foundation* +
_Establishes the compliance rails, Maven POMs, security configs, and GitOps pipelines._

STEP 2::
*Generative AI / LLMs = The Engine* +
_Accelerates development of specific business logic, data mappings, and algorithms inside the methods._

STEP 3::
*The Production Service = The Result* +
_A production-ready microservice wrapped and protected by enterprise-grade infrastructure._

By initializing projects via the SVC Generator, developers get a production-ready, fully compliant framework within minutes. They can then safely utilize AI to rapidly implement the actual *domain business logic* inside that secure, pre-configured framework.
